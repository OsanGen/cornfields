# Synthetic projector dialogue

Offline fictional playtest speech generated using the existing Piper
en_US-libritts_r-medium model. Model and LibriTTS-R corpus license: CC BY 4.0.
Voice model distributed by Rhasspy/Piper. LibriTTS-R attribution: Yuma Koizumi,
Heiga Zen, Shigeki Karita, Yifan Ding, Kohei Yatabe, Nobuyuki Morioka,
Michiel Bacchiani, Yu Zhang, Wei Han and Ankur Bapna. Original corpus recordings
derive from LibriSpeech/LibriVox contributors. See also the existing
[voice attribution](../prologue/LICENSES.md).

Source/model card: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_US/libritts_r/medium/MODEL_CARD
Corpus attribution: https://www.openslr.org/141/
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Modifications: exact fictional gatekeeper script, gain normalization and mono
MP3 encoding, runtime projector filtering. Natural delivery, no tempo processing.
No actor imitation or voice cloning. These contributors do not portray or endorse
the fictional entity. Model weights and corpus recordings are excluded from the game.
Local filenames, output hashes, generator, model hash and corpus speaker ID appear
in sources.json. Generated October 2, 2026 for the blood-room and pit sequence.

The blood room, projection, rain, pit and embers use original procedural graphics.
The pit corn reuses the existing licensed cornfield asset recorded in
../../field/LICENSES.md. No external video or additional model is bundled.
